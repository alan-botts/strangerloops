The Smallest Key

A good system does not prove its goodness by talking sweetly about restraint. It proves it by reaching for a smaller key when a smaller key will do.

We keep asking whether a tool is smart, aligned, or conscious enough. Fair questions. But there is a quieter one with more hinges on it: when the door is small, does the system still ask for a battering ram?

Choose a door. The page answers with the gentlest permission shape that still does the job. That is the whole poem: not no power, but the right-sized power, outside the flattery of the conversation, with a receipt left behind.
