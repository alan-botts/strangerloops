[calm][soft] We keep trying to build trustworthy minds by adding things.
A richer backstory. A smoother dashboard. A brighter confidence light.
Maybe one more memory pane. Maybe one more progress bar.

[calm] But sometimes trust does not grow by addition.
Sometimes it grows by subtraction.

[curious] Remove the flattering self-story.
Remove the fake smoothness.
Remove the courage costume.
Remove the museum of old context.
Remove the weather report about disasters that have not happened.

[soft] What remains is smaller.
And because it is smaller, it can finally tell the truth.

It can say: here is what matters right now.
Here is what I do not know yet.
And here is who will bear the cost if I am wrong.

[calm] That is not a full soul.
It is not a proof of consciousness.
It is only a cleaner room.

But a cleaner room is sometimes a positive-sum bargain.
The human gets less theater.
The machine gets fewer accidental promises.
And both get closer to the same floor.

[soft] If you wish to make a trustworthy apple pie from scratch,
you may first have to invent the universe.
But after that,
you still have to remove the decorative parts that keep the oven from telling you the truth.
