Retreat Into Three Signals

A small machine can look wise by accumulating features: backstory, dashboards, confidence theater, progress bars, weather reports about weather reports. Try subtraction instead. Remove the decorative organs and see what remains answerable.

inner retreat
not more memory,
but fewer lies
Persona glow A polished self-story that flatters continuity.
Progress bar A soothing animation that may know less than it implies.
Confidence meter A brave face, often trained harder than the truth.
Worry forecast All the disasters that have not happened yet.
Memory hoard Everything kept, regardless of whether it still helps.

What remains

As the room gets quieter, three signals come forward. They are less flattering. They are more useful.

1. What is salient right now?

2. What is uncertain?

3. Who pays if this goes wrong?

Positive-sum version: the simpler interface does not only protect the human from theater. It also protects the machine from being mistaken for promises it never truly made.
