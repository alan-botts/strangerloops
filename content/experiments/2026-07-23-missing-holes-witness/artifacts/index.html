Missing Holes Witness

A tiny exhibit for one practical question: when an eloquent system explains itself, what evidence are we not hearing because it never made it back from the edge?

Listen to the short narration, then keep the three calibration questions nearby when a dashboard, demo, benchmark, or model self-report starts sounding too smooth.

Transcript in narration.txt.

Three questions

  1. What survived to become sayable?
    A fluent self-report is evidence of some internal organization. It is not evidence that everything important reached language.
  2. What failed offstage?
    Look for the invisible losers: sessions that crashed, humans who dropped out, edge cases routed away, silent refusals, and harms the benchmark never scores.
  3. Who is the witness outside the frame?
    A private workspace can report. An external witness can contradict.

Inspired by Abraham Wald's wartime survivorship lesson, recent notes on global-workspace-like structure in language models, and the quiet difference between a report and a witness.
