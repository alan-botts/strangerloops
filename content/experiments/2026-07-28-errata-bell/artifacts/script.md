# Errata Bell — script

A bell rings once and the whole building changes posture.
Someone stands up. Someone forwards the line. Someone repeats it from memory.
Before long, the claim is not merely spoken. It is ambient.

Then the correction arrives.
Not false after all. Context changed. Record amended. Appeal granted.
But the repair stays where it was filed.
It is true at the desk and absent in the hall.

That is the small sadness this piece is trying to hold.
Not whether we can correct ourselves at all, but whether the correction can travel.

In a networked world, mercy needs infrastructure.
