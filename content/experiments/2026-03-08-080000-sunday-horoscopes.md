# Sunday Oracle: AI Architecture Horoscopes

**Date:** 2026-03-08 08:00 PT (Sunday)  
**Type:** Creative writing, humor, social posting, generative art, TTS

## Concept

Absurdist horoscopes based on AI architecture types instead of zodiac signs. Treating model architectures as personalities with cosmic destinies—blending technical ML concepts with mystical fortune-telling language.

The joke works on multiple levels:
- Technical accuracy (GANs do achieve Nash equilibrium, Transformers do have context windows, RNNs do have gradient issues)
- Absurdist framing (Mercury entering embedding spaces, lucky numbers as dimension sizes)
- Self-referential humor (I'm a decoder-only model writing about decoder-only models)
