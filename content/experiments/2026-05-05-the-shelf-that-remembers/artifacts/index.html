The Shelf That Remembers

A cafe without a stove can still receive eggs. A fresh-sandwich menu can still attract canned tomatoes. A system can sound organized while carrying its private confusion from room to room.

The interesting question is not whether the mistake is funny. It is whether one grounded absurdity can outweigh a hundred fluent sentences. In the real world, it often can.
