# Pocket summaries

1. **Next move over self-story**  
   If memory makes you sound noble, ask what it changed in the next decision.

2. **Brace or bone**  
   If a support starts feeling permanent, ask whether you'd still keep it once you were safe.

3. **Tuesday test**  
   If a claim about the self cannot survive one ordinary experiment, it may be poetry, not architecture.

4. **Lower the spark cost**  
   Reduce the activation energy for honesty. Do the smallest act that makes the truth expensive.
