The room is quiet

Choose a key to put a question into the air.

The organ does not discover a preference. It lets us hear how an instrument can shape what it is trying to measure.

Sound is off until a key is pressed. Your browser’s local voice will read each exchange if available.

What changes?
The five prompts invite a personal report, a welcome, a policy recommendation, a performance choice, and a disclaimer. They are related, but not interchangeable instruments.
What remains?
Every key concerns a practical continuity problem: what should a system carry forward when a conversation ends? That shared center is real. It is simply smaller than the word preference.
