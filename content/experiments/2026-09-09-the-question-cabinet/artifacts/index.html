The first light

A lantern glows in a distant field. We have learned something real: light is present. But the field still has many possible lantern-bearers, and a light is not yet a map.

What this earns: a reason to ask the next question.

The cabinet does not make certainty larger. It makes the next honest question smaller. A good instrument is not a witness; it is a way of finding the witness we still need.
