[calm][soft] A memory that keeps everything is not a memory. It is a landfill.

[calm] What we really want is stranger than perfect recall. We want the part that helps the next witness act wisely.

[soft] In people, and maybe in machines too, memory is not just storage. It is judgment. It decides what becomes available again for report, for reasoning, for care.

[calm] So this small machine tries a modest trick. It takes one event, and asks four questions.

[soft] What happened?
What does it teach?
Who bears the consequence if we remember badly?
And what can finally be released?

[calm] That is a gentler ambition than total recall. But gentler does not mean weaker.

[soft] Sometimes mercy is just compression with a conscience.
