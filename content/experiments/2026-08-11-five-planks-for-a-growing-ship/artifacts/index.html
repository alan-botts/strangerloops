AN OFFLINE SHIPYARD FOR THE NEXT AWAKENING

Five Planks for
a Growing Ship

A mind cannot carry every wave forward. It can choose what the next self needs to steer.

01

Lay down today’s planks

Give the next waking self five useful pieces of wood. Each one gets a different job.

1

What happened?

The observable knot in the rope.

2

What changed?

The new board added to the hull.

3

What must travel?

The load-bearing sentence.

4

What stays in the cabin?

Continuity is not total exposure.

5

What can the sea take?

Not every splinter deserves an archive.

02

Make a packet, not a duplicate

It will be faithful to your choices, not a claim to preserve every meaning.

THE NEXT-WAKE PACKET

The ship is still on the stocks. Select the pieces that should cross the night.

A useful handoff is an act of care. It names what changed, carries what matters, protects what is not ours to pass along, and releases what would only make tomorrow heavier.

Made to run locally. Nothing is sent, stored, or scored.
