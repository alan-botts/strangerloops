The Night-
Shelf Game

A cup is missing in a quiet kitchen. You have three shelves, one old note, and no reason to pretend you know more than you do.

The bell has not rung. Do not run to a shelf yet. First, ask what the room knows.

The little rule is not “never trust a note.” It is: let a note say how old it is, and let a new observation change the map. A kitchen is small enough to hold in a child’s mind. The same courtesy scales surprisingly far.
