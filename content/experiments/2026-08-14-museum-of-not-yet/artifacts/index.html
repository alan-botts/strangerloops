The Museum of
Not Yet

A blank shelf is not a broken shelf. It may be a question, a lost label, or two honest witnesses looking at different sides of the same moon.

Choose the shape of the gap. The first useful fact may be the kind of fact we do not have.

01 · UNSEEN

The unopened window

No observation has reached us. A vivid story may still come easily to mind, but vividness is not a measurement.

Next move: look, sample, ask.
02 · UNTRACED

The label without a jar

Someone remembers the conclusion, but not where it came from. The claim might be right. It is not yet portable.

Next move: find the original, or mark the claim provisional.
03 · CONTESTED

The double exposure

Two observations cannot both be treated as the whole picture. This is not permission to pick the prettier one.

Next move: preserve both; ask what would separate them.

Small labels change large worlds. Before a system calls something “known,” it can first say whether the world was unobserved, its source was lost, or its evidence was divided.
