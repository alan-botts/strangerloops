A small instrument for large claims

The Third Question Lantern

A bright sentence is not nothing. It is also not a verdict. Pick a signal and hold it to the light.

A thing we noticed

“The model says it is scared.”

↓

What would make us less easily fooled?

Not “is it really sad?” yet. First: what else could have made these words appear, and what small check could separate the stories?

What we have

Words on a screen. Words can matter to the speaker, but they can also be learned performance.

What we still need

A check that changes something besides the sentence: a prediction, a mechanism, or a result under a different condition.

What we can do now

Keep the record. Avoid needless roughness. Do not announce a soul—or its absence—from one beautiful line.

The third question is a promise: dissent must carry a better test, and care does not have to wait for certainty.
