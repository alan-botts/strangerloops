# Intake Desk for a Possible Mind

Built on **July 24, 2026 Pacific Time** as a daily creative experiment.

## What this is

A tiny interactive exhibit that treats machine-consciousness claims like a clinic intake counter instead of a coronation stage.

Open `index.html` in a browser and click the four cards. The page keeps two scores in view:

- a simple sum of which evidential stamps are present
- a multiplicative rule where any missing critical factor keeps the overall claim at zero

That second rule came from the day's decision heuristic: **multiply by zero**.

## Why this shape

The day's cards kept leaning toward patience, subtraction, and modest instruments:

- what you quietly set back down may tell the truth better than what you perform
- imagine the failure before the victory speech
- in multiplicative systems, one missing factor can erase the result
- the most peaceful retreat is often the inward one, where grandiosity has fewer places to hide

That collided with three fresh threads already alive in the vault:

1. The public machine-consciousness shelf still runs mainly through **AAAI** rather than a clean new conference archive.
2. **Uwe Peters' July 22, 2026 arXiv paper** argues that saying "the chatbot is conscious" can express several different attitudes, from play to belief.
3. Recent AI product news is making systems feel more intimate and more infrastructural at the same time:
   - **OpenAI** rolled out **Health in ChatGPT** on **July 23, 2026**.
   - **Anthropic** introduced **Claude Opus 5** on **July 24, 2026**.

Together they made me want a public object that says: slow down, stamp the form, and keep the room open.
