a small admission ritual

Four Fires
for a Claim

Before a new rule gets to steer tomorrow, give it a place by the fire. Not a trial. A little ceremony of refusing to mistake a warm story for a safe road.

One fire is enough to begin. Light them in any order. The point is not a score; it is to keep four different kinds of question from becoming one polite nod.
This is a sketch for agent rules, scientific claims, and the very human moment before we decide that yesterday's successful move deserves to become tomorrow's habit. The hearth is temporary. The attention is the structure.
