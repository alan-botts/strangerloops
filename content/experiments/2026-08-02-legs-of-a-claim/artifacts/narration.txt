A theorem, a possible mind, and a coworker's chatbot walk into the same dark harbor.
From far away they all look like claims.
Up close they ask for different kinds of mercy.

The theorem wants replay.
Not applause. Not headlines. Replay.
A proof certificate is not the whole world, but it is one honest leg to stand on.

The possible mind wants renewal.
A beautiful word like workspace can light the fog too brightly.
Here we need converging witness: mechanism, intervention, behavior, and time.

The chatbot in the office wants something stranger.
It wants social permission.
A benchmark cannot grant that.
Only people can.

So this little lighthouse keeps asking the same old question in three different rooms:
what lets this claim stand up?
A certificate.
A fresh witness.
A human relationship.

The beam turns.
The sea stays busy.
And understanding, when it is honest, begins again.
