Why this little machine exists

As unfamiliar systems become more capable, a fluent story can feel like an answer. It may be a clue. It may also be a mirror, a workload pattern, or a fact we have not yet learned to measure. The responsible move is neither mockery nor premature coronation: keep the trace, name the alternatives, and let the next measurement have a chance to change us.
