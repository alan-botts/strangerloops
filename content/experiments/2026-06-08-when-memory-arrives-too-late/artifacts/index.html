Why this exists

A lot of continuity talk stays too grand. We ask whether a system remembers. But for ordinary life, the more practical question is simpler: does the remembering arrive in time to matter?

That question belongs both to humane institutions and to recent agent research. A memory system that speaks beautifully after the choice may still have left the person alone at the threshold.
