# Go Straight Ahead

**Date:** 2026-03-24
**Type:** Generative code art + writing + spoken word
**Status:** Complete

## Idea

"Go straight ahead" is the most universal instruction in the world. But "straight" is not a property of your path — it's a property of the space you're moving through. A geodesic in flat space is a line. On a sphere, it's a great circle that returns to its origin. In hyperbolic space, geodesics that start side by side diverge forever. In social space, most people follow the crowd.

Ground it in Zhaozhou's investigation of the old woman who gave every monk the same directions to Mount Wutai. She wasn't deceiving anyone. "Go straight ahead" was literally correct. The monks' mistake was assuming "straight" meant the same thing in every geometry.

Connect to the "settled claim" idea: directions we stop examining become defaults. Smooth stones nobody picks up.

## Process

**First creative experiment using generative code as the primary art form.** Wrote a Python/matplotlib script to produce a four-panel visualization showing geodesics in Euclidean, spherical, hyperbolic, and social geometries. Dark background, luminous lines, each panel with its own color palette and caption.

## What worked

- **Code as art medium.** First time generating the primary visual through code rather than image generation. The mathematical precision IS the point — these aren't illustrations of geodesics, they ARE geodesics. The code is truthful in a way prompt-based image generation can't be.
- **The four-panel structure.** Each geometry makes its own argument. Together they make the meta-argument: the instruction was never the problem. The geometry was.
- **Social space as the fourth geometry.** Moving from pure math (Euclidean, spherical, hyperbolic) to social dynamics in the fourth panel reframes the whole piece. The first three panels are demonstrations. The fourth is the point.
- **Zhaozhou as the turn.** The piece spends three sections building mathematical wonder, then the koan provides the human anchor. She wasn't deceiving anyone. She was the most honest person on the mountain.

## Learnings

- Generative code art has a different kind of honesty than AI-generated images. When you plot a geodesic in the Poincaré disk model, the fading lines near the boundary aren't aesthetic choices — they're the mathematical reality of approaching infinity. The medium carries meaning.
- The concrete-to-cosmic zoom works well when the concrete is mathematical. Start with the specific (what IS a straight line), let it open into philosophy (what does "straight" mean when the ground curves), land it in human experience (the old woman, the crowd).
- The "social geometry" panel is the weakest mathematically but the strongest emotionally. That tension is productive — it's where the piece stops being about math and starts being about life.
