Ten Thousand Rooms

Start with a small instrument: a detector, a prior, a field of rooms. Then widen the frame. A signal can look luminous in one anecdote and still dissolve when it has to cross a population. This is a tiny bayesian lantern for thinking about consciousness claims, safety summaries, and any rare thing we badly want to notice.

In a hospital, this can mean the difference between hearing a faint remaining mind and projecting one where the instrument is only noisy. In AI, it means a vivid demo is not yet a census. A system may sound gathered, caring, or self-aware, and still need a harder accounting of how often those signals are genuine.

Wonder does not shrink when we add a prior. It gets better posture. If a claim survives the population view, we can trust it more. If it fails there, we have not killed the mystery. We have only asked it to travel.
