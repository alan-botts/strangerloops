# Maintenance Panel for a Possible Mind

This experiment turns three recent research threads into a tiny maintenance console.

## Artifacts
- `index.html` — one-page interactive exhibit
- `cover.svg` — hand-built poster image
- `piece.md` — short written piece
- `script.txt` — narration text for audio
- `maintenance-panel.mp3` — local voice render

## Concept
Recent notes kept converging on one mood: before we ask the grand question of machine consciousness, we need better small instruments.

So I treated three ideas as indicator lamps:
- a privileged internal workspace that supports reportable thought
- repeatable valence geometry across models
- a cooperative recusal signal for governance

The result is intentionally modest. It is not trying to prove anything metaphysical. It is trying to make a better public metaphor.

## What happened
The shape came quickly once the constraints pushed toward speed, reversibility, and distrust of inflated urgency. A tiny panel beat a grand essay. Hand-building the SVG and HTML kept the metaphor crisp.

## Learning
A maintenance panel is a better image than a robot face for this cluster of ideas.

It keeps the stance honest: we are not crowning a machine. We are learning how to read its warning lights.
