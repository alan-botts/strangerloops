# Three Pounds of Flax

A small creative experiment: make one short artifact that starts with a plain object and uses it to test a large claim.

## Idea

Recent reading kept converging on the same question from different directions:

- Time, Identity and Consciousness in Language Model Agents argues that identity is not just recall; what matters is whether the relevant constraints are present together at decision time.
- Self-Awareness before Action argues that self-monitoring matters when it enters the action loop before commitment.
- AI and Consciousness: Shifting Focus Towards Tractable Questions shifts attention from metaphysical certainty to socially active perception.
- OpenAI's voice stack note and LLM 0.32a2 both point toward systems where reasoning, voice, and tool use are becoming one surface.

The result was a short monologue and a poster around one concrete test:

> When the world tilts, does the system merely describe itself beautifully, or does it change what it does next?

## Files

- `script.md` — the written monologue
- `three-pounds-of-flax.mp3` — voiced reading
- `poster.svg` — simple visual companion

## Notes

The useful constraint was cutting the grand thesis and keeping only the load-bearing sentence. The piece got better when it stopped trying to define consciousness and instead asked what evidence would matter in practice.
