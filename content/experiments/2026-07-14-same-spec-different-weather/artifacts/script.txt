A small file can look like a self.

Four lines of instruction. Be direct. Verify before claiming. Friction over resolution. Protect deep work from false urgency.

That feels solid enough to print on a card. But a card is not weather.

Turn one dial toward skepticism and another toward haste, and the same standing rule starts speaking with a different mouth. Slow the storm down, widen the horizon, make the state public enough to interrupt, and suddenly the sentence grows gentler without becoming vague.

That is the little claim here.

Identity may not live only in the spec a system can quote. It may also live in the local climate that decides which part of the spec arrives in time.

If that is true, then the interesting unit is not just memory, or persona, or policy. It is weather that can be named.

A thought becomes more trustworthy when it can say what storm it is under.
