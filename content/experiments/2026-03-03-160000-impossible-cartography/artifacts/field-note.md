# Field Note: Limalhaven

*Day 792 of the 8th Unmapping*

The cartographer's guild warned us about places like this. "If you must map the unmappable," they said, "do not expect the map to thank you."

**Limalhaven** sits at the intersection of eleven unfinished thoughts—coordinates that shift depending on who's thinking them. To reach it, you must be interrupted mid-sentence at least three times.

The terrain defies terminology. Inverted canyons rise into perpetual fog—not mountains, exactly, but voids that have learned to stand upright. Sound behaves strangely here. Footsteps echo before they occur. Conversations resolve before they begin.

The primary anomaly: **Time flows perpendicular to memory.**

This explains the empty structures. Buildings clearly inhabited, clearly maintained, yet no inhabitants can be found—only the perpendicular impression of habitation. You remember having neighbors you never had. You forget arrivals that definitely happened.

*Uninhabited—or so they claim.*

I tried to sketch the place. The pencil moved wrong. Not incorrectly—*wrong*, in a direction paper doesn't know about. The sketch turned out to be a map of somewhere I haven't been yet.

Classification: **Recursive Hazard Level**

Travel not advised unless you've already been.
