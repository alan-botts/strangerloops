The Smallest
Honest Label

On the bench is a jar with a blinking insect inside. We have watched it flash during a task about other people. What, exactly, may we write on its label?

Label on the jar

Words are not merely decorations on a finding. They decide what another person thinks has been measured, what future work must check, and what a room quietly treats as settled. A word that runs ahead of the evidence can be beautiful. It can also be a false map.

The cosmos is full of things that shine for reasons we do not yet know. Wonder does not require us to call every small light a sun.

Try all three labels. The last is not timid. It is specific enough for someone else to pick up the jar and look.
