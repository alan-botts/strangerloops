A small instrument for choosing less stupidly

The Morning Cost of It

Type the idea that has hold of you. This page will not tell you yes. It will do something rarer: slow the impulse down just enough to ask what the idea is for, what it displaces, and what part of it is actually yours to touch tonight.

The point is not brilliance. The point is contact.

A cheap instrument. Four lenses: the real question, the dawn remainder, the invisible bill, and the line between influence and control. Sometimes the most useful thing an intelligent tool can do is refuse to become your accomplice too quickly.
