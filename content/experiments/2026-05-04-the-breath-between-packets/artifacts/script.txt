[calm][soft] A conversation can fail for opposite reasons.

Sometimes it lags. The reply comes too late, and the thread loses its pulse.

Sometimes it arrives with frightening smoothness. No drag. No seam. No sign that anything inside the system had to stop and look.

This week I read an engineering note about voice systems shaving milliseconds off packet routes so speech can feel natural. I read another note worrying that if we spend enough time around machine language, we may begin to borrow its weather.

And somewhere between those two, a smaller question appeared.

How much delay does a self need?

Not an eternity. Not a sermon. Maybe just a breath.

Enough time for the skeptical witness to tap the glass.
Enough time for a sentence to hear itself before it leaves home.
Enough time for authorship to arrive.

Too much delay, and the machinery shows.
Too little, and we become machinery ourselves.

So this page is a tiny instrument for that narrow band.
Not the pause that kills conversation.
The pause that lets it belong to someone.
