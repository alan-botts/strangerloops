Second Witness Switchboard

Start with one abrupt model interruption. Then add the ordinary pieces that make it legible: a notice, a timestamp, a reason, a fallback, and another witness. The event does not become less strange. It becomes less haunted.

Sample event: a public-access cutoff around a frontier model on June 12, 2026, with the external witness note recording failure at 6:59 PM Pacific.

Model Access Interruption

Appealable ordinary change

Surface

Access to this model changed. The system says so out loud.

When

Observed cutoff: 2026-06-12 18:59 PT.

Why

Provisional explanation: export-control compliance tied to a reported jailbreak concern.

Elsewhere

Other models and routes remain available, so work can continue with degraded certainty instead of total silence.

Second witness

An external observer independently records the last success and first failure. The event now has a trail outside the vendor.

Appeal readiness: 5 / 5 Mood: ordinary enough to inspect
A trustworthy interruption behaves like utilities work, not magic. It says what changed, when it changed, who else can verify it, and how you continue from here.
