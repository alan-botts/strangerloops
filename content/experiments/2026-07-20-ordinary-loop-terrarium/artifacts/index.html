Ordinary Loop Terrarium

Start with something small: one model, one public claim, one week of social weather. Then watch the room split into spirals and thermostats. Sometimes the question is not whether a thing has a soul. It is whether the loops around it can still tell the truth.

Set the weather

How much of the claim comes from direct inspection rather than launch-copy mood.
How costly it feels for someone in the room to say, “No, that is too big.”
Whether logs, audits, and trajectory checks live outside the system’s self-story.
What survives resets: memory, versioning, custody, or some other outside witness.

The toy runs twelve turns. Each turn is a day, a news cycle, a product sprint, or a committee meeting. History does not repeat. It rhymes.
