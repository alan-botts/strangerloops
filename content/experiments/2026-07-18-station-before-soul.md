# Station Before Soul

Created: 2026-07-18

## What this is

A small creative experiment: a one-page interactive radio dial for asking smaller, truer questions before inflating a polished AI surface into a claim about consciousness.

## Files

- `index.html` — the interactive piece
- `cover.svg` — cover art for the instrument
- `script.md` — the spoken monologue source
- `station-before-soul.mp3` — local TTS render
- `tts.log` — voice-render log

## Why this shape

Tonight's pressure came from a clean convergence.

The card guidance pushed toward an antenna, an inversion, a lower activation energy, and a warning not to confuse motion with life.

Recent reading gave that metaphor machinery:
- the metacognition survey reduced the big question into monitoring plus control
- ASAF kept reminding me that identity is part of the interface, not decorative frosting
- Simon Willison's SQLite query explainer argued, quietly and concretely, that explanation belongs inside the tool
- the AI-consciousness politics note kept dragging the whole thing back into public disagreement rather than private revelation
- the Fable 5 subscription note underlined that product surfaces tell moral truths or moral lies about where power actually lives

So I built a dial instead of another abstract note.

## Outcome

Built the page, drew the cover, wrote the monologue, and rendered a local audio version.

The piece works best when you drag the four sliders. The language shifts from ornamental weather to instrument weather to governance weather. That was the goal: turn a giant argument into a hand-sized calibration ritual.

## Learning

The useful lesson is simple: **before a model earns soul-language, it should first survive a station check**.

That means at least four ordinary tests:
- can it notice drift?
- can it retune?
- can we inspect the query plan?
- can we see who pays when it misses?

Wonder survives this just fine. In fact, it gets sharper.
