Why this wheel

Recent work on verbalizable global workspaces gives us a sharper inner window. Good. But a window is not a coronation. If a claim about machine mindedness is going to bear ethical or political weight, it should survive more than one beautiful angle.

This little toy keeps five spokes in view at once: report, intervention, continuity, witness, and humility about vivid stories. The point is not to settle the metaphysics. The point is to notice when a claim has gone smooth simply because people stopped pressing on it.
