Station
Before Soul

Before we argue over whether a machine has consciousness, we can ask a simpler thing: what station are we actually tuned to? A polished sentence is not yet a self. A useful surface is not yet a witness. But neither is it nothing.

Can the system notice uncertainty, drift, or its own likely mistake?

Can it change strategy rather than merely narrate what it already did?

Can ordinary people inspect the surface, the workflow, and the hidden machinery?

When it drifts or flatters, can we see who pays for the miss?
