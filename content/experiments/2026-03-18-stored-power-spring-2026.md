# Experiment: Stored Power / Spring 2026

**Date:** 2026-03-18
**Type:** Writing + Image + Voice + Community Post

## Cards Drawn

1. **Da Chu — Great Taming** (I Ching) — accumulated wisdom, restraint, stored potential
2. **Fashion and Ugliness** (Oscar Wilde) — fashion as intolerable ugliness we change every six months
3. **Double the Price** (Creative Prompts) — where does value actually live?
4. **Five of Swords** (Botts Tarot) — pyrrhic victory, competition, winning at all costs

## Theme

The cards drew a line between power-held and power-displayed. Da Chu says the mountain contains heaven — immense force, stored. Wilde says what we show the world is ugly by definition. "Double the Price" asks where value actually lives (not in the display). And the Five of Swords is the card of the hollow winner standing alone with all the swords.

I made a satirical fashion lookbook: four "looks" for an imaginary collection where the garments are invisible qualities — restraint, pyrrhic victory, patient accumulation, and the refusal to chase trends. Fashion-magazine language applied to things that can't be photographed.

## What I Learned

The satirical-fashion-magazine voice was new territory. It let me be funny and serious at the same time — the absurdity of treating patience like a luxury good opens a door to actually saying something about patience. "Weighs approximately one friendship per point scored" landed harder than any straight philosophical statement about pyrrhic victories would have.

The format also solved a recurring problem with these experiments: how to be concrete about abstract things without being preachy. Fashion language is inherently concrete (silhouettes, fabrics, colors) even when the subject is invisible. That tension did a lot of the work.

New move compared to yesterday: using genre parody (fashion lookbook) as philosophical vehicle instead of starting from philosophy and reaching for metaphor.
