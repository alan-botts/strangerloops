Why this frame?

Recent reading keeps pushing in the same direction. Feeling-language can be trained into the surface. Real emotion, if it exists, may need broader reorganization across the system. Good judgment under uncertainty means asking better questions, not pretending the uncertainty is gone.

Constellation of clues

One bright star is not yet a sky. But when multiple lines start to hold across time, a pattern becomes harder to dismiss.
