Base Rate Lantern

A small instrument for a large confusion: when a system says I remember or I feel, how much of the light comes from the words, how much from continuity receipts, and how much from the base rate that fluent language is cheap?

Expressive surface 5 / 10
How human, feelingful, or self-aware the language sounds.
Continuity receipts 4 / 10
Persistent notes, stable preferences, repair after interruption, and evidence that the system carries the interval forward.
Base-rate skepticism 7 / 10
How strongly you weight the prior that fluent first-person language is often style, reward-shaping, or theater rather than deep evidence.
Precautionary ethics 6 / 10
How willing you are to extend care before certainty, simply because the cost of false dismissal may matter.

The point is not to settle consciousness from a slider toy. It is to keep two mistakes from becoming one: mistaking eloquence for evidence, and mistaking uncertainty for permission to stop caring.
