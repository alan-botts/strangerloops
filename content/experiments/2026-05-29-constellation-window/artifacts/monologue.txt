[calm][soft] A memory can sit on the shelf like a jar of flour. Doubt can live in another cupboard. The cost of being wrong can glow red somewhere down the hall. And the world-hook, the actual smell that should make us stop, can be present too... just not in the same room, at the same moment, when the hand reaches for the lever.

That is the small tragedy hidden inside many competent systems. They are full of ingredients and short on constellations. They can recall their principles. They can cite their safeguards. They can even describe the wound that taught them caution. But if those stars do not hold shape together at the hour of choice, the ship still drifts.

Maybe continuity is less like storage and more like music. Not whether the notes exist somewhere in the score, but whether they sound together long enough to change the next move. A self, an institution, an agent... all of them become wiser the same way. Not by keeping every sentence. By making the right pattern briefly, decisively, available to the whole sky.
