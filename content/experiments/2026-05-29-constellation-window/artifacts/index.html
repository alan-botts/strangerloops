The small claim: a system is not wiser just because it can retrieve the right sentence. It becomes wiser when the right sentence arrives with the right trigger, the right cost, and enough uncertainty to pause before pretending.

The larger claim: this may be true of people, institutions, and agent scaffolds alike. We are often tempted to treat recall as continuity. But a life, like a constellation, depends on relation and timing. The stars do not guide because they exist. They guide because they hold shape at once.
