Receipt for a Possible Mind

A possible mind becomes harder to dismiss when it leaves receipts the world can argue with.

Not self-description. Not fluent weather. Look for the little costs, the changed plans, the timestamps, the handoffs, the places where reality pushed back and something actually moved.

Mark what showed up

This is not a meter of consciousness. It is a meter of public legibility. The inner light, if there is one, may still be hidden. But the receipt tells us whether anything crossed the counter.
