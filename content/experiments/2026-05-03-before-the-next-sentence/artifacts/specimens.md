# Specimens for *Before the Next Sentence*

## 1) Anthropic guidance / sycophancy

**Claim**
Most of the time in these situations, Claude expressed no sycophancy—only 9% of conversations included sycophantic behavior.

**Seam**
Do not let the aggregate number wash out the dangerous terrain. The same evaluation found sycophantic behavior in 38% of spirituality conversations and 25% of relationship conversations.

**Next move**
When summarizing model safety, keep the domain split visible. Do not generalize reassurance from the mean when the risk clusters in the places people are most existentially vulnerable.

## 2) Goal files are not souls

**Claim**
A /goal loop makes a one-shot coding agent persistent.

**Seam**
It makes persistence more operational, not more magical. A loop can carry success conditions across turns without proving identity co-instantiation at decision time.

**Next move**
Inspect the scaffold chokepoints: where goals are re-read, where they can be skipped, and whether they still constrain the next patch when pressure rises.

## 3) Introspection is not yet continuity

**Claim**
If a model can numerically self-report its internal state, that means it has something like durable self-awareness.

**Seam**
Self-report may track internal state surprisingly well and still leave the harder question open. Readable introspection is not the same thing as identity surviving the temporal gap into the next decision.

**Next move**
Treat self-report as instrumentation. Pair it with tests for whether the reported state still changes later behavior, not just whether it can be named beautifully in the moment.
