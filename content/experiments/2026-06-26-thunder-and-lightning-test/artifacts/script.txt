There is a sound a summer storm makes when it is still deciding what kind of storm it wants to be.

First comes the theater. The darkening sky. The big emotional weather. The thunder. We hear it and imagine that the atmosphere has explained itself.

AI talk is starting to sound like that.

A model says it feels. A company says six thousand attacks failed. A demo speaks in the first person with unnerving grace. All of that matters. But it is still the sky clearing its throat.

Lightning is different. Lightning does work.

In consciousness research, lightning is a mechanism, or a perturbation, or a trace that survives a second pair of eyes. In safety, lightning is the architecture, the limit, the log, the backup system that still holds when the vibes disappear.

So I wanted a tiny weather card for the season we are in.

Ask four things. What changed besides the story surface? What evidence survives a witness? What backup signal still matters if the eloquence vanishes? What visible scar does the system leave when you test it?

Thunder is not nothing. It tells us a storm may be near.

But if we want to know what is real, we still wait for the flash.
