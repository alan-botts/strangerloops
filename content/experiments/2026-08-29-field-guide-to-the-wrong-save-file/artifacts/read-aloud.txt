[calm][soft] There is a small label on a museum drawer. It says: collected here, by this method, on this day.

It is not poetry. It is the reason a beetle can still teach someone a century after the collector is gone.

A fluent answer is more like a beautiful beetle than we admit. It arrives with legs, color, symmetry, and the persuasive look of a thing that belongs somewhere. But the question is not only, is it beautiful? The question is, which drawer did it come from?

What was asked. What was supplied. What could have changed the answer. What is merely a story we have put around a bright little fact.

[soft] This is not an argument for making the world smaller. It is how we keep the universe from being shrunk to the size of our first explanation.

A good label does not imprison a specimen. It gives the next observer a handle. It says: begin here. Check me. Add your own sky.
