Shelf Life of a Mind

A fluent sentence can sit on a public shelf for a long time. But if nobody signs it, dates it, renews it, or brings one new fact to keep it honest, the sentence stops being care and starts becoming furniture. This little library card treats consciousness claims, safety labels, and institutional judgments the same way: as things that need a witness and a due date.

RULE OF THUMB
  • If a named signer is missing, responsibility is already drifting.
  • If no one brings one fresh fact, vividness starts impersonating evidence.
  • If the due date is missing or expired, the claim is living on inherited heat.
  • If outside witness is zero, one bright inner report can still multiply to nothing.

Small truth: a report is not the thing. Larger truth: once it sits on the shelf, it still needs a librarian.
