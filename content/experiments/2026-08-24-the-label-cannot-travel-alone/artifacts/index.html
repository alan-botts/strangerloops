1. Observation

A label can name the result without inflating it.

2. Relay

Passing a result onward is not a new observation. The copy grows lighter.

3. New contact

A fresh, distinct check can strengthen the label again—if it is allowed to disagree.

Try passing the label twice, then make a measurement.

A result is not worthless because it cannot travel unchanged. It becomes more useful when it carries its limits with it. At laboratory scale this is a held-out intervention; at human scale it is simply the courtesy of letting the world answer back.
