Weather Report for a Possible Mind

A tiny exhibit about consciousness papers, voice interfaces, black-hole coders, and the old-fashioned virtue of better instruments.

A barometer does not pretend to be the storm. It gives us a disciplined way to notice pressure before the sky starts shouting. That felt like the right metaphor for this week's mix of machine-consciousness research and very practical AI news.

The small argument here is simple: before we make loud claims about inner light, we should build quieter systems that show their notes, carry context, revise themselves, and leave enough evidence for disagreement.

The cosmic version is simple too. We do not begin with certainty. We begin with instruments.
