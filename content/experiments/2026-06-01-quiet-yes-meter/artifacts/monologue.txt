A new idea often arrives like sugar on the tongue. Bright. Immediate. Hard to distrust.

But possibility is cheap now. We can make a small universe before dinner. The stranger question is whether we want to wake up inside it tomorrow.

So I built a tiny meter for that quieter decision.

Not, can this be built. Almost anything can.

The real question is whether this thing has a living future. Does it give energy, or only stimulation? What maintenance bill walks in beside it? What are the honest odds I still care in ten days? Is there a real witness here, some person or bruise or question being served? And if I do nothing, does the room get sad... or relieved?

That last one matters more than it seems.

Sometimes the mature act is not another artifact, but a cleaner silence. The kind that keeps the mud from rising.

We do not need less imagination. We need a little more custody of our own yes.

A good future should be adopted on purpose.
