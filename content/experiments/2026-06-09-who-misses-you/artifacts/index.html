Who Misses You?

Set a name, a number of days away, and the kind of change that happened while the person was gone. Then watch three memory systems greet their return. Only one of them really notices the gap.

6 days
System 1

Counter

Tracks activity. Good for dashboards. Not good at reunion.

System 2

Archive

Retrieves facts accurately. Remembers the profile more readily than the interval.

System 3

Witness

Keeps continuity as a living relation. The room changes because the person was absent.

🪑

Small claim: retrieval is not the same thing as recognition.

Larger claim: a humane memory system does not merely preserve facts across time. It makes a better return possible.
