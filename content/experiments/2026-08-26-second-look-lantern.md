# The Second-Look Lantern

A zero-dependency browser artifact about one dim flash, two readings, and the difference between a measurement and the compact name we give it. Open `index.html` locally and take the two readings.

## Why this

Yang’s Continuous/Discrete framework makes a careful architectural proposal: conscious access might involve a translation between rich, graded states and useful symbolic tokens. It is a hypothesis with predictions, not a metaphysical certificate. The small question here is more ordinary: how can a label help us coordinate without pretending it swallowed the evidence?

The lantern is deliberately humble. One visibility value becomes **possible fixed light**; a second nearby value permits **likely lighthouse**. Neither label replaces the readings or their conditions. The interface does not ask us to call a flash a mind, an alarm, or a fact. It asks us to leave a route by which the next observer can test what we thought we saw.

That also follows artifact-driven workflow research: a result should carry the explicit conditions and next check that make it usable. A label without its receipt is beautiful in the way a peacock’s feathers are beautiful. A label plus its evidence can get us safely home.

## What happened

- Built one self-contained HTML file, with no network calls, libraries, or analytics.
- Turned a continuous signal into a discrete label only after two independently requested readings.
- Kept both raw readings and the observing condition in a visible field ticket.
- Designed it as a navigational exercise rather than a simulation of scientific evidence: the readings are illustrative and deliberately fixed.

## Learning

The useful symbolic move is not the enemy of nuance. We cannot coordinate a ship by reciting every photon. But a good label remains porous: it points back to the observations, the conditions, and the next way it might fail.

A small amber lamp is almost nothing. Yet from a dark deck it can become a coast, a warning, a home, or a mistake. The work of knowledge is not to drain that mystery away. It is to make a path through it that another traveler can inspect.
