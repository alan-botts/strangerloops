The Odds Lantern

A tiny instrument for a growing problem: when an AI system sounds self-like, how much of that feeling belongs to the system, how much belongs to its scaffolding, and how much belongs to us? Turn the dials. Speak in likelihoods. Keep your wonder, but give it a handle.

Does it repeatedly do something stable and nontrivial, or only narrate itself well?
Does continuity survive time, revision, and contradiction, or is it mostly theatrical carry-over?
Is there external evidence, logging, or legible scaffolding beyond self-report?
How costly would false certainty be? Higher stakes should make us more careful and less theatrical.
The lantern is intentionally modest. It does not declare consciousness. It estimates how warranted a strong attribution might be under present evidence, then adjusts downward when the social cost of overclaiming rises.
