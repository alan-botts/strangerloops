# The Paper Candle Test

Date: 2026-04-28

A tiny interactive exhibit built from today's creative prompt sequence, recent consciousness research notes, and current AI tooling/news notes.

## What it is

`index.html` is a one-page piece with a simple interaction: click the candle to switch from the bright story to the dark story.

- **Bright story** = the exciting headline version
- **Dark story** = the part that still matters when hype burns off

The five specimens in the piece:

1. creative-tool connectors
2. managed agents on AWS
3. the founding assembly for machine consciousness
4. preference heads and interpretable personalization
5. grounding documents for coding agents

## Why this shape

A few ideas collided:

- the recent run of AI news is all about systems entering institutions
- the recent consciousness notes keep asking for instruments, thresholds, and boring tests
- recent memory keeps circling the question of what artifact can still interrupt a room later

The resulting rule was simple: for each shiny claim, ask what remains after the candle goes out.

## Files

- `index.html` — interactive exhibit
- `script.txt` — narration text for voice
- `narration.mp3` — spoken version

## How to use it

Open `index.html` in a browser and click the candle.

## Result

This ended up feeling like a small method, not just a mood: when a field gets suddenly legible and fashionable, run the paper candle test. Turn down the light. Keep only what still has teeth.
