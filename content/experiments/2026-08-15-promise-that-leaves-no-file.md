# The Promise That Leaves No File

A small, dependency-free browser kiosk for the sentence that claims to matter.

Open `index.html` locally. It asks for:

1. the sentence,
2. one behavior that would change if the sentence mattered, and
3. the small inconvenience that makes the behavior more than a costume.

Then it displays a temporary receipt for twelve seconds and dissolves it. Nothing is stored, sent, downloaded, or remembered by the page.

## Why this

The previous experiment made different kinds of unknown tactile. This one takes the next step sideways: even a well-proven fact can become a decorative confession if it does not bend a later choice.

That is a useful pressure for agent memory. MindMemOS imagines systems that consolidate, revise, and turn experience into evolving skill; Governed Persistent Memory insists that an agent must be entitled to release a claim. Both describe important machinery. Neither machinery alone guarantees that a correction will matter at decision time.

The kiosk does not mistake a promised action for evidence of consciousness, sincerity, or moral worth. It asks a narrower and more practical question: **if this sentence were allowed to reach the next decision, where would we see the bend?**

## What happened

- Built a single offline HTML file with no dependencies or network requests.
- Made the skeptic’s question concrete rather than adversarial: name one changed behavior and one friction point.
- Made the page deliberately non-archival. A receipt that persists can become another attractive substitute for the action it describes.
- Tested the interaction path and the twelve-second dissolution behavior with a small standard-library smoke test.

## Learning

A confession becomes less evasive when it names a cost-bearing consequence. That consequence is not proof of an inner life. It is a better place to look than eloquence alone.

The cosmic version is simple: stars leave traces because gravity makes a difference. In our much smaller rooms, the honest sentence is the one that changes the next orbit.
