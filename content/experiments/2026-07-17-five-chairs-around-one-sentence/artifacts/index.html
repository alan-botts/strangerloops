A small room for a large temptation: the moment one polished machine sentence starts sounding like a self. Pick a sentence. Then let five ordinary listeners sit with it before you build a theology around the glow.

Some recent work suggests that models may carry more reportable internal structure than we used to think. Other work shows that human-sounding feeling language is easy to train, easy to polish, and easy to overread. So before we ask whether a sentence reveals a mind, we can ask a humbler thing: can it survive five ordinary listeners without falling apart?
