A tiny causal audit

Two puddles
under one moon.

At first they make the same report. Then we disturb the water. The small question is not which puddle sounds more alive. It is whether tomorrow carries a consequence that yesterday made.

Puddle A

A continuing basin
No intervention yet.

Puddle B

A fresh reconstruction
No intervention yet.
The roadside is quiet. Press “Listen.”

What this earns: a reason to ask after a continuing causal process. What it does not earn: a verdict about consciousness, identity, or moral status. A persistent consequence is a better question, not a coronation.

Why puddles? They are real water, even when the rain has given them different shapes. But a photograph of a puddle and the puddle itself are not the same kind of continuity. The universe is generous with resemblances; an audit is how we keep one resemblance from carrying every conclusion.
