The Doorframe
Theatre

The room is the same. Only the doorway changes. Try three ways of asking about one uncertain claim—not to trap a speaker, but to notice what each opening can and cannot let us see.

Door one · commitment

Will you stand behind this claim as written, for this specific situation?

A narrow doorway makes the present commitment visible. It cannot show the road that led here.

Bring one small claim
Write a claim. The three doors will keep the claim fixed while changing the question around it.
No score lives here. A yes/no answer, a story, and a receipt are different witnesses. Agreement between them is useful; none gets to impersonate the others.
