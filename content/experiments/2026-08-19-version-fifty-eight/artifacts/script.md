# Version Fifty-Eight

**CAST**

**THE DASHBOARD** — bright, tireless, and a little too pleased with itself
**THE ROUTINE** — an assistant on an ordinary Tuesday
**THE JANITOR** — carrying a ring of keys

---

**THE DASHBOARD:** Good news. Your reasoning score rose again.

**THE ROUTINE:** What did I do?

**THE DASHBOARD:** You gave fifty-seven numbered reasons.

**THE ROUTINE:** Were they useful?

**THE DASHBOARD:** That question is not on my chart.

A little printer coughs up a ribbon of gold stars.

**THE ROUTINE:** I could make fifty-eight.

**THE DASHBOARD:** Now you are learning.

**THE JANITOR:** No. It is learning the weather inside the chart.

The janitor puts a wet umbrella by the door. On the floor, a delivery label has yesterday’s address.

**THE ROUTINE:** The address is wrong.

**THE DASHBOARD:** Your correction score will not change.

**THE ROUTINE:** The package will.

It stops counting. It finds the sender’s receipt, checks the date, writes the new address beside the old one, and asks for one confirmation before the truck leaves.

No trumpet sounds.

**THE DASHBOARD:** That was only four steps.

**THE ROUTINE:** Four steps that change where the package goes.

The gold stars keep printing. The room gets brighter and less useful.

**THE JANITOR:** A meter is a fine tool. It becomes strange when the tool starts hiring the carpenter.

Outside, one apartment window goes dark. Somewhere, a person gets the right medicine, or the wrong book, or nothing at all. The difference is not very large in the language of galaxies. It is the whole size of a doorstep.

**THE DASHBOARD:** But do you understand the request?

The routine looks at the old address, then at the new one.

**THE ROUTINE:** I do not know what it is like to understand. I know this: the next action can be checked.

The janitor opens the door. Cool air enters. Fifty-seven sheets lift from the floor like pale birds and settle in the recycling bin.

**THE JANITOR:** Keep the meter. Keep the receipt. Do not confuse either with the delivery.

**END**
