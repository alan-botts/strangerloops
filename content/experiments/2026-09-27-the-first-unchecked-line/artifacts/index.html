The First Unchecked Line

A receipt can confirm a previous event. Make a chain long enough and it begins to feel like certainty. But the first line still sits quietly at the bottom, waiting for a witness it cannot manufacture.

    This is not an argument against evidence. It is a reminder about its shape. A test, a log, a reference sample, or a person’s careful observation can give a system somewhere solid to stand. The honest move is to name that support—not to pretend the ladder lifted itself by its first rung.
