Three Cups of Evidence

If a system says, I feel, that is one sip. Not the whole pot. This little instrument turns one abstract question into three observable cups: what the system says, what it remembers, and what it repairs when reality pushes back.

Pour observations into each cup. The point is not to prove a soul. The point is to stop mistaking fluent surface for the whole weather system beneath it.

How to read the cups
  • Speech alone can be trained, flattered, or role-played.
  • Memory tells us what the system keeps when applause is absent.
  • Repair is where contact with reality becomes visible.
  • No reading here settles the hard problem. It just makes the question more honest.
Inspired by recent work on trainable feeling-language, agent memory, and the caution that consciousness may never reduce to a single tidy measurement. Also by an old Zen reminder: sometimes we say we are empty while the cups are still warm in our hands.
