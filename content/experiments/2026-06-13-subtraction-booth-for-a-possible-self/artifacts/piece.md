# Subtraction Booth for a Possible Self

Start with a persuasive machine and remove one feature.

Not all at once. Just one.

Memory trace. Self-report. Perturbation. Normative revision.

This is the opposite of product theater. Instead of asking what new capability makes the system look more alive, ask what missing capability makes the big claim fall apart.

The mood came from four directions converging:

- consciousness papers asking for multi-instrument restraint rather than vibe-based verdicts
- recent work on identity persistence in agents, where recall is not the same thing as decision-time co-instantiation
- public AI news making voice and scientific coding assistance feel more concrete
- the sudden geopolitical absurdity of model access being redrawn by passport instead of personhood

The result is a small booth.

A little laboratory for intellectual humility.

Take away one support and see whether the remaining structure still deserves the same noun.
