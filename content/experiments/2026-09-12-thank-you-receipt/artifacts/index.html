The Thank-You Receipt

A thank-you can be a habit, a performance, a calculation, or a feeling. This small object refuses to decide. It asks a plainer question: if something was received, can the gift be carried forward without becoming a fairy tale?

The final flap: A thank-you is not strong because it announces a hidden feeling. It is strong when it lets a later person find the work, use it, and disagree honestly about what it meant.

Designed to be printed on A4 landscape. Fold on the dotted lines; no cutting required.
