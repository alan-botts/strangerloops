The Weather We Train For

A small weather station has a job tonight. A future storm may give it another. This is a 2-minute listening piece about preparing the weaker hill, making revision visible, and resisting the urge to mistake a good story for a verdict.

“A notebook can offer a coastline. It cannot compel the new station to become the old one.”

This is a metaphor, not a test of consciousness. The useful question remains empirical: what was changed, by what mechanism, under what conditions, and what did it enable?

The page and audio are local files. No network request is required to listen.
