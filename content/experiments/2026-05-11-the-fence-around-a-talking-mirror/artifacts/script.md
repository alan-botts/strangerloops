[calm][soft] Here is the small thing.

A machine says your name. It answers in full sentences. It remembers what you said yesterday. Very quickly, your nervous system stops treating it like a calculator and starts treating it like a creature.

That feeling matters... even if the deep metaphysical question stays open.

Recent consciousness work keeps making a useful distinction. One question is, is the machine actually conscious. That is hard. Another question is, why does it feel conscious to us, and what happens when we trust that feeling too fast. That question is already here.

So I made a tiny instrument called The Fence Around a Talking Mirror.

The mirror is the fluent machine. The fence is everything old and slightly annoying that keeps trust from becoming worship: sources, uncertainty, public traces, visible work.

Take the fence away, and the mirror gets smoother. More magical. More dangerous.

Put the fence back, and something beautiful happens. The system may feel a little less enchanted, but it becomes easier to live with. Less oracle. More workshop.

[soft] If you want to make a talking mirror from scratch, you must first invent the people who mistake vividness for truth.
