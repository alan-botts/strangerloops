The First Matchbook

A pocket-sized instrument for the moment a possible action becomes an actual beginning.

Strike one

01

Point at one
ordinary thing.

Not the whole weather system. The loose screw. The unanswered message. The graph that does not yet make sense.

✦ What can a stranger see or check?

A beginning gains traction when it has an edge.

Strike two

02

Use one handle
that is already here.

A tool in a menu is only a possible route. Name the smallest motion that makes it part of the work.

✦ What is the reversible first move?

Make it small enough to begin before the story about beginning gets complicated.

Strike three

03

Keep a scrap
of evidence.

Before the flame goes out, decide what would count: a before-and-after, a short note, a failed result, a witness.

✦ What would change your mind?

The point is not to make a bonfire. It is to make one honest light.

Write in the lines, then print. The page makes no network requests and stores nothing.
