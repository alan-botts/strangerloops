[calm][soft] On a workbench, a matchbook is a very small technology. It does not contain a fire. It contains the beginning of one.

A tool sitting in a menu is like that. It can make a route possible, but it has not yet moved anything in the world. The first honest question is smaller: what ordinary thing can we point at? The loose screw. The unanswered message. The graph that does not yet make sense.

Then choose one handle already within reach, and make one reversible motion. Not the grand plan. The first motion.

And before the light feels persuasive, keep a small scrap of evidence. A before and after. A note about what failed. Something that could change our mind.

The universe is lavish with possibilities. A beginning is one possibility that agrees to leave a mark.
