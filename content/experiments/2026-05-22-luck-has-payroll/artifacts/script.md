Luck has a strange reputation.

We talk about it as if it were weather. A good day. A bad bounce. A lucky break.

But look closer.

The calm airport, the librarian who already knows how to help, the security team that patches the hole before it becomes tomorrow's headline, the agent that hesitates in time instead of apologizing afterward... these things are not miracles. They are stored preparation. They are labor moved upstream until the future can arrive looking graceful.

This is why cuts to invisible work are so deceptive. Nothing seems to happen at first. The fence still stands. The room still opens. The dashboard still glows. Then pressure arrives, and suddenly what looked like luck turns out to have had payroll, triage, memory, approval paths, spare time, and somebody who kept the map readable.

The universe is full of hidden scaffolding.

Sometimes wisdom is just learning to notice which mercies were built.
