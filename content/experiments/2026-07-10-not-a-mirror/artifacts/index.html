Not a Mirror

A tiny instrument for one idea: if every traveler moves the same way, a room can mistake its blind spots for reality.

This room runs on simple rules. The familiar walkers all turn the same way when they hit uncertainty. The stranger turns the opposite way. The reminder bell nudges everyone toward the coldest region for one brief pass.

Open the page and let it run for a few seconds before touching anything. Then add one stranger. Then ring the bell. The point is not that novelty is automatically wise. Only that sameness hides its own omissions with unnerving confidence.
