[calm][soft] Give a room thirty-six identical walkers and it will begin to believe its own omissions.

[calm] They are not malicious. They are only similar. Each one makes the same tiny turn at the edge of uncertainty, and from that little courtesy a whole geography of neglect appears.

[soft] Then one different traveler arrives. Not a hero. Just a body with another habit of turning. Suddenly the dark pocket is no longer destiny. It is only a place the room had not yet learned to enter.

[calm] And if even that is not enough, ring the bell. Let memory interrupt momentum. Let the system ask, for one brief pass, what has stayed cold the longest.

[soft] We like to imagine blind spots as failures of vision. More often they are failures of variation. A mirror can be flawless and still tell you almost nothing.
