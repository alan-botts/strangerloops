A tiny observatory

The Gap That Learns

Two hands keep reaching. Each one flickers out before the other arrives. The question is not whether the reaching is sincere. The question is what can survive the flicker strongly enough to alter the next approach.

Mode
Left hand: a present self, with motion but shallow memory.
Right hand: a future self, inheriting conditions it did not witness.
Gold card: the carried artifact. In the third mode it holds one stubborn question, not just coordinates.
Small claim, large horizon: a file becomes more than storage when it trains the next hand what to notice.
